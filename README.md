# Swiggy SQL Case Study | Business Analysis

## Project Overview
This project uses SQL to explore Swiggy's food-delivery operations through 13 business questions covering customers, restaurants, orders, and delivery partners.

The objective is to demonstrate how SQL can answer practical business questions and support data-driven operational decisions.

## Tools & Technologies
- SQL
- Joins and aggregations
- Subqueries
- GROUP BY and HAVING
- Date functions
- DISTINCT and conditional filtering

## Business Questions

### Customer Analysis
1. Identify customers living in Delhi.
2. Find the average restaurant rating in Mumbai.
3. List customers who have placed at least one order.
4. Count the total orders placed by each customer.
5. Identify customers who have never placed an order.
6. Count orders placed by each customer in Mumbai.

### Restaurant & Order Analysis
7. Calculate total revenue generated by each restaurant.
8. Find the five restaurants with the highest average rating.
9. Retrieve orders placed within the most recent 30-day period relative to the dataset's latest order date.

### Delivery & Customer Behavior
10. Identify delivery partners who completed more than one delivery.
11. Find customers who placed orders on exactly three different days.
12. Identify the delivery partner who worked with the greatest number of distinct customers.
13. Find customers in the same city who ordered from the same restaurants on different dates.

## Key Skills Demonstrated
- Combining related tables using joins
- Counting orders and distinct customers
- Aggregating revenue and restaurant ratings
- Filtering grouped results with HAVING
- Analysing order dates and repeat behaviour
- Comparing customer and delivery-partner activity
